Shop Alerts

Your wishlist, checked against the shop. Everything below is worked out on this device each time you open the page — no account, no sign-up.

How alerts work: add skins to your wishlist with the ♥ button anywhere on FortShop, then check here. Items in today’s shop appear first, then wishlist items whose expected return date falls within the next 7 days. Expected dates are estimates from tracked shop history — Epic does not announce returns in advance. Email and text alerts aren’t live yet; for now, this page is your alert board.
