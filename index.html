Fortnite Item Shop FAQ

What’s in the Fortnite item shop today?

Everything listed on this page. FortShop shows every skin, bundle, emote, wrap and Jam Track in today’s Fortnite item shop with live V-Bucks prices, updated at every shop rotation — and Today’s Top Picks highlights the day’s best returns and brand-new items.

When does the Fortnite item shop reset?

The Fortnite item shop resets every day at 7:00 PM Eastern Time. The countdown at the top of this page shows exactly how long is left until the next rotation, and you can switch it to your own timezone if you’re on the West Coast or anywhere else.

When is a skin coming back to the Fortnite item shop?

Epic Games doesn’t announce return dates in advance, so nobody knows for certain. FortShop tracks every skin’s shop history and estimates its return from its usual gap between appearances — search for any skin on the Search page to see when it was last seen and when it’s expected back, or open the Predictions page for the most overdue items right now.

What are the rarest Fortnite skins?

The rarest Fortnite skins are the ones that have been away from the item shop the longest. Our Rarest page ranks skins and cosmetics by days since their last appearance, and the items marked Crowned — like Renegade Raider and Aerial Assault Trooper — have no shop appearances in our tracked records at all, so there is currently no way to buy them.
