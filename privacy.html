Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

FortShop (“we,” “us,” “the Site”) is a free, unofficial fan-made Fortnite item shop tracker at sportstalk24.com. This policy explains, in plain language, what information the Site does and does not collect, how cookies and advertising work, and the choices you have. By using the Site you agree to this policy.

1. We do not ask for your accounts or personal logins

FortShop never asks for, and you should never enter, your Epic Games, PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo, Google, or any other account username or password on this Site. We do not collect payment information. We do not sell personal information.

2. Information stored only on your device

Your wishlist, timezone choice, and similar preferences are stored in your own browser’s local storage on your own device. That information is not transmitted to us, is not tied to your identity, and you can delete it at any time by clearing your browser site data or using the “Clear wishlist” control on the Wishlist page.

3. Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) and, if enabled, analytics and advertising partners may automatically receive standard technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and timestamps. We use this only to operate, secure, and improve the Site and to measure overall traffic in aggregate.

4. Cookies and advertising

The Site itself sets no tracking cookies for its core features. If advertising is enabled on the Site, third-party advertising partners — which may include Google AdSense and other ad networks — may use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies to serve and measure ads, including personalized ads where permitted by law and by your choices.

  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings and at aboutads.info/choices.
  • Visitors in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland will be shown a consent message, where required, before personalized ads are served.
  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time; the Site’s core features will continue to work.

Some links or codes on the Site may be affiliate links or an Epic Games Support-A-Creator code. If you use one, we may earn a commission or a percentage of a purchase at no additional cost to you. This never changes the prices or information shown on the Site.

7. Children’s privacy

The Site is a general-audience information service and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under GDPR and California law), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or limit the use of your personal information, and to opt out of its “sale” or “sharing” for advertising.
